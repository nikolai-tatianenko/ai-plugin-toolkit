# CL-004: Source-linked fact and decision extraction

Status: implemented
Milestone: v0.1
Dependencies: CL-002, CL-003

## Scope

Extract validated candidates with source spans, scope, proposal/decision distinction, and idempotent writes.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every accepted claim has a resolvable source span; invalid citations are rejected; reruns do not duplicate facts.
- Record verification commands/results and relevant source or build revision.
- Document unsupported cases and remaining work; do not mark verified from mocks alone.

## Limitations and remaining work

- A claim quoted verbatim from injected text passes validation (the quote exists). The evaluation harness flags it as unsupported; human review is the safeguard (ADR 0005).
- Statement paraphrase quality is not checked. Extracted facts stay `candidate` until `loom memory confirm`.
- No contradiction review, forgetting or retention (CL-013).
- Deterministic results use the recorded fixture; live extraction quality is unmeasured.
